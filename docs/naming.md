# Naming

Research as of 2026-09-26.

| Candidate | Idea | GitHub repos with that name | Domain | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Presserl** ⭐ | Austrian diminutive of *Presse*: "the little press" | 0 | presserl.at / .com without DNS | ✅ unique, available, friendly |
| Kinderpresse | descriptive ("children's press") | 0 | kinderpresse.at without DNS | generic, hard to protect |
| Druckfrisch | "hot off the press" | 0 | druckfrisch.at taken | ❌ domain gone |
| Extrablatt | classic newspaper term ("special edition") | 5 | — | ❌ crowded (cafés, newspapers) |
| Klecks | "ink blot" | — | — | ❌ existing school newspaper and family magazine |
| Schreiberling | "little scribbler" | — | — | secondary meaning "hack writer" |

Decision: **Presserl**. (In Austrian dialect *"an Presser haben"* means "to be in a hurry" — fitting for a deadline.)

Names of individual newspapers belong to their forks, not to upstream.
